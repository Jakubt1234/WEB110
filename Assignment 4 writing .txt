In this lesson, I learned about electronic mail, also known as email, and how it is used to communicate and exchange information over the internet. Email is an important communication tool because it allows people to send messages and files quickly to other internet users. I learned how to use an email application to create a new message and add the correct recipient addresses. I also learned that it is important to write emails clearly and professionally. Before sending an email, I should check my spelling, edit my message, and make sure the information is easy to understand. I also learned how to create and use an automatic email signature.
Another important skill I learned was how to attach files to an email when necessary. Attachments can include documents, pictures, or other types of files. I learned how to open attachments from received messages and save them to the appropriate folder on my computer. The lesson also taught me how to manage received emails. I learned how to reply to messages and forward them to other people. I can also search for specific emails, sort messages, and save important emails using different settings. I also learned that email can include security concerns. It is important to adjust email account settings to help restrict or quarantine potentially dangerous messages. This can help protect my computer and personal information from security threats.
Finally, I learned how to determine the priority of an email before sending it and how to print an email when needed. Overall, this lesson helped me understand the basic features of email and how to use them effectively. These skills will be useful for school, work, and everyday communication because email is a common way to share information, communicate with others, and send important files.
